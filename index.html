Rules Summary

Campus dress code rules

Use this summary as a quick printable reference for respectful university appearance.

👔

Dress neatly

Choose clothes that are clean, ironed, and suitable for an academic setting.

👞

Wear safe shoes

Closed-toe shoes are preferred for safety and a more formal appearance.

🧥

Keep it modest

Avoid very short, sleeveless, torn, or overly casual clothing.

✨

Stay tidy

Maintain clean grooming and an organized appearance throughout the day.
